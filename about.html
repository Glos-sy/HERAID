HERAID is more than an organization; we are a movement dedicated to reducing maternal mortality and ensuring every woman in Nigeria has access to safe reproductive healthcare.

Our Purpose

We exist to bridge the gap in healthcare access and provide the support systems necessary for safe motherhood.

Our Mission

To drastically reduce maternal mortality rates in Nigeria through comprehensive education, direct access to prenatal care, and community empowerment initiatives that put women's health first.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every birth is safe, every mother is supported, and reproductive healthcare is a fundamental right accessible to all, regardless of location or economic status.

Core Values

The principles that guide our every action and decision.

Empathy

We lead with compassion, understanding the unique struggles of every woman we serve.

Innovation

Finding new, effective ways to solve old problems in low-resource settings.

Community-Led

Solutions built by the community, for the community, ensuring sustainability.

Transparency

Open and honest in our operations, funding, and impact reporting.

Our Journey

From a small community initiative in Lagos to a nationwide movement, here is how HERAID has grown over the years.

Read full annual reports →
2018

The Inception

Founded by Dr. Ada Obi after witnessing the preventable loss of life in rural clinics. Started as a volunteer group of 5 doctors.

2020

First Mobile Clinic

Launched our first mobile clinic van, allowing us to reach remote villages in Ogun State, providing prenatal vitamins and checkups.

2022

Partnership Expansion

Partnered with 15 local hospitals to subsidize delivery costs for low-income women. Reached our milestone of 5,000 lives touched.

2024

National Recognition

Recognized by the Ministry of Health as a key partner in reproductive health. Launched the "Safe Birth" digital app for remote consultation.
