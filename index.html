Our Core Mission

We believe that no woman should lose her life while bringing life into the world.

Maternal mortality in Nigeria remains one of the highest in the world. At HERAID, we are bridging the gap by providing accessible, high-quality maternal healthcare to underserved communities across the nation.

  • ✓
    Education & Advocacy

    Empowering women with knowledge about reproductive rights and health.

  • ✓
    Direct Medical Support

    Providing free screenings, prenatal vitamins, and clean birth kits.
